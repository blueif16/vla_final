Does the robot read the instruction?

A learning path on vision-language-action models, from what they are to the question our project asks about them.

Jingyi Fu and Shiran WangCS 6180 final project

We're Jingyi Fu and Shiran Wang, and our CS 6180 project is about , the networks that let a robot look through its cameras, read an instruction and move its arm. These chapters are how we learn the field, and they're written so anyone, or anyone's AI assistant, can learn it with us. Each chapter opens on one concrete case, defines every term where it first appears, and ends with questions to check yourself.
